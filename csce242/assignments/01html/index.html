The Tyranasour Rex VS The Spinosaurus

T. rex was a large, powerful dinosaur that lived during the late Cretaceous Period. It had a huge head, strong jaws, and sharp teeth that helped it catch and eat other animals. T. rex walked on two strong legs and had a long tail that helped it balance while moving. It also had very small arms compared to the rest of its body.

Spinosaurus was a large dinosaur with a long, narrow snout and a unique sail along its back. It had longer arms than T. rex and large claws that may have helped it catch prey. Its long, paddle-like tail suggests that it was well adapted to spending time in and around water. Spinosaurus had a body shape that was very different from T. rex, showing how dinosaurs could develop different features to survive in different environments.

Quick Facts

  • T. rex lived during the late Cretaceous Period, about 68 to 66 million years ago.
  • Spinosaurus is thought to be the largest known carnivorous dinosaur, even bigger than T. rex.
  • Both dinosaurs were theropods, meaning they walked on two legs and were mostly meat-eaters.
  • Spinosaurus fossils have been found mainly in North Africa, while T. rex fossils are mostly found in North America.
