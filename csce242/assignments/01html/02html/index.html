The Knights

The Knights of the Round Table were a group of legendary knights who served King Arthur in the kingdom of Camelot. They were known for their courage, loyalty, and skill in battle. The Round Table represented equality because no knight was considered more important than another. Some of the most famous knights included Sir Lancelot, Sir Gawain, Sir Galahad, and Sir Percival. Together, they went on quests and defended Camelot from enemies and other dangers.

The knights were also known for their adventures and quests for honor and glory. One of their most famous quests was the search for the Holy Grail, which tested their courage and purity. Sir Galahad was considered one of the most noble and pure knights and was successful in finding the Grail. Sir Lancelot was one of the strongest knights, but his relationship with Queen Guinevere caused conflict within Camelot. The stories of the Round Table continue to be popular because they focus on bravery, friendship, loyalty, and honor.
